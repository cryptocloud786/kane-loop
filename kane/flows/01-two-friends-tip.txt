# Two friends, uneven order, 10% tip
Add a person named Asha and a person named Ben. Add an item called Pizza with price 600 shared by both.
Add an item called Soda with price 100 shared only by Ben (uncheck Asha before adding it). Set Tip % to 10.
Verify that the Owes column shows 330.00 for Asha and 440.00 for Ben, and the Bill total is 770.00.
