# Removing a mistaken item updates every total
Add a person named Asha and a person named Ben. Add an item called Pizza with price 600 shared by both.
Add an item called Cake with price 200 shared by both. Then remove the Cake item from the list of items.
Verify that Cake is no longer listed, the Owes column shows 300.00 for Asha and 300.00 for Ben, and the Bill total is 600.00.
